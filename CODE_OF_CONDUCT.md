# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We build in public. Engagement here should be technical, honest, and direct.

- Be precise. Vague praise and vague criticism are both useless.
- Disagree with ideas, not people.
- If something is wrong, say so — with a reason.
- Don't overclaim. Shipped and verified beats "planned" and "aspirational".

## Not welcome

- Personal attacks or harassment of any kind.
- Deliberate misrepresentation of the project or its contributors.
- Spam, off-topic promotion, or automated noise.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may close issues or remove comments that violate these norms
without further explanation. Repeat offenders are blocked.
